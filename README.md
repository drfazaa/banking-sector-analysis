# Banking Sector Analysis
## Objective:

To evaluate listed Indonesian banks and determine which companies present attractive long-term investment opportunities based on financial performance, valuation, and macroeconomic conditions.

## Research Question:
- Which bank has the strongest fundamentals?
- Which bank has the highest ROE?
- How sensitive are earnings to interest rates?
- Which bank offers the best risk-adjusted return?

## Companies

- Bank Central Asia (BBCA)
- Bank Rakyat Indonesia (BBRI)
- Bank Mandiri (BMRI)
- Bank Negara Indonesia (BBNI)

- Annual Reports
- Quarterly Reports
- Financial Statements
- Investor Presentations
- Economic Data
